Step 1 — Detect USB WiFi adapters
Runs lsusb + iw dev. Flags known monitor-mode-capable chipsets (Alfa AWUS036NHA, Panda PAU09, TP-Link TL-WN722N v1, etc.)
Step 2 — Enable monitor mode
Runs airmon-ng check kill + airmon-ng start <iface>. Creates a <iface>mon interface for passive capture and injection.
Step 3 — Live discovery scan
Runs airodump-ng for N seconds and parses the CSV. Shows all APs and clients in range.
Step 4 — Capture WPA handshake
Deauth injection is illegal against networks you don't own. Leave "Deauth client MAC" empty for passive capture only.
Step 5 — Crack the captured hash
Once Step 4 captures a handshake, the hc22000 hash appears above. Copy it into the ⚡ REAL Cracker tab, or use hashcat locally: 
hashcat -a 0 -m 22000 hashes.hc22000 /usr/share/wordlists/rockyou.txt
